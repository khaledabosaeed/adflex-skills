# AdFlex brand

If the content repo has `brand/brand.md`, it is newer than this copy. Use it.

## Logo

- «Ad» #3292FF blue, «Flex» #9848FE purple.
- Big sparkle gradient #C95AC5 → #55AAFF. Small sparkle #F83AAE → #D453C2.
- Use the logo from the brand kit (`get_brand_kit`). For end cards, use a high-resolution rebuild; don't upscale the small PNG.
- Get a real SVG or high-res logo from AdFlex before running paid ads.

## Colors

| Use | Value |

| ALFA accent / caption box | #8738F5 |

| Dark text | #1C1824 / #333 |

## Fonts

- **Arabic:** Cairo (ExtraBold for captions, Black for titles) from npm `@fontsource/cairo`, or IBM Plex Sans Arabic for ALFA UI. The Arabic subsets have no ASCII digits, so set numbers in the Latin font.
- **Latin display:** Archivo Black, Unbounded Black, Anton, Playfair.

## Captions

White Cairo ExtraBold on a rounded purple (#8738F5) or dark box at the bottom, phrase-timed to the measured speech. Wrap mixed Arabic/Latin lines in RTL embedding.

## End card

- The AdFlex logo is the hero.
- AdFlex works inside **the AI you already use**: Claude and ChatGPT chips plus an MCP / «+» badge, all small and equal weight.
- Never «أي موديل» or «أي ذكاء اصطناعي».
- Approved lines:
  - «اربطه بالـ AI اللي بتستخدمه»
  - «شغّال جوّا المساعد اللي بتستعمله»
  - «حملتك كاملة، بليلة وحدة»
  - «ابدأ حملتك الحين»
- End with adflex.ai.
- Rejected, don't bring back: a «Claude × AdFlex» lockup as the logo; «بيشتغل مع» with a «وأي AI» chip; «مش بس جوّا Claude… وكمان جوّا ChatGPT» (it implies only those two).
- Claude and ChatGPT are examples. The message is "the AI you already use"; a round «+» badge covers the rest.
- Layout and timing of the card are in `editing-lessons.md`.

## Tone

Confident, fast, a bit cheeky. The speaker is a creator showing a friend something cool, not a corporate narrator. Dialect (Levantine or Gulf) to fit the video, never MSA.

## Third-party marks

Check OpenAI's brand guidelines before showing the OpenAI knot or "GPT Image" in paid ads, and confirm an integration is live before claiming it.
